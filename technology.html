    FAQ

    Questions we hear often

    What does a session feel like?

    Steady warmth and a firm, rolling massage along your spine. It should not hurt, and you can ask for the intensity to be changed at any time.

    How long is a session and how many will I need?

    Each session is 40 minutes. Dr Ncube will recommend how many you need after your consultation.

    Is it safe for everyone?

    Not always. Pregnancy, pacemakers, fever, open wounds and some heart conditions may need a doctor's approval first. That is why we start with a consultation.

    What should I wear and bring?

    Wear light, comfortable clothing. Bring a list of any medication you take, and drink some water beforehand.

    Can I use this alongside my usual treatment?

    Yes. We see it as a supportive therapy. Keep taking prescribed medication and keep your own doctor informed.
